Training certificate

Editable certificate issued to each trainee who completes the session and quiz.

  • Certificate templateName, role, facility, training dates, trainer signatureLink pending

Logos to include

  • Qure.ai
  • Ministry of Health / National TB Programme
  • Funding partner (e.g. Global Fund, Stop TB Partnership / GDF)
  • Procurement or implementing partner (e.g. PFSCM, UNOPS, local NGO)
  • Device partner where relevant (e.g. MinXray)

Confirm logo usage with each partner before printing. Use the latest approved versions.

07

Training & installation report

Completed at each site and signed by the end user to confirm handover.

  • Report formatWord template — fill one per siteLink pending

The report should cover

  • Site, district, region and date(s) of visit
  • Devices installed: serial numbers for laptop, X-ray, dongle / Starlink
  • Software versions and licence activation confirmed
  • Test scans run and results visible on dashboard
  • Trainees: name, role, attendance, quiz score
  • Open issues and agreed next steps
  • End-user name, designation, signature, facility stamp and date
  • Qure.ai trainer name and signature
